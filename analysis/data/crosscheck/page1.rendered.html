Draper University, in partnership with
Cardano and Draper Dragon, introduces the DraperU Cardano Accelerator Ecosystem Programs. We are seeking high-impact Web3 founders to drive global adoption on the Cardano blockchain. Join us in Silicon Valley for an intensive elite residency, strategic capital, and direct access to institutional VCs and the global Draper network.

All participating startups gain direct access to, and strategic support from, the newly minted $80M Draper Dragon Orion Fund to supercharge their institutional scale.
Who Are We Looking For?
We invest globally. You do not have to be based in the USA to qualify. Everyone is encouraged to apply. Please note that this is an in-person program, so you must be eligible for a US visa.
If you're building a Web3 startup in any of these verticals or anything else and would like to hear from us, we'd love to hear from you.

FAQs

-

Are there any fees? What is covered?

There are no fees to participate in the Cardano x DraperU Genesis Pre Accelerator. However, you will need to pay for your own transportation (flights, rideshare, etc.) and meals. We will provide accommodation and occasional meals, which will be mentioned in the program calendar.

-

Is this program in person or remote? What language is it taught in?

This program requires all participants to be in-person at Draper University (San Mateo, CA) for the duration of the program.

-

Can I build on multiple chains?

This program is in partnership with Draper Dragon's Orion Fund, which aims to back builders on Cardano. You must be building on Cardano as your main chain, although multi-chain projects will be considered.,This program is also a good opportunity for builders to implement Cardano further into projects, which may be accepted with milestones in place.

-

Do we get an investment? What are the terms?

Not all companies are guaranteed to receive an investment from the Orion Fund. After being selected for the Pre Accelerator, we will put you through a 4-week diligence period where our investment team will make final decisions.,Selected teams may receive up to $20,000 in investment for a target of 2% equity.

-

Is food provided? Do I have to organize my own food?

Some meals will be provided during catered networking events. For day-to-day meals, you'll need to make your own arrangements. If you need additional support, feel free to reach out—we'll do our best to help throughout the program.

-

Do I need to be based in the USA to qualify?

No, you don't need to be located in the USA to apply. We welcome applicants from all over the world. However, you must be eligible for a US visa. Our team will assist you with support letters and necessary documentation to help with this process.
