This site is VerifyCardano, the Cardano Regulatory Evidence Portal operated by Intersect MBO. Its seal and wordmark exist so readers can recognize the site and so anyone who cites it can point to it clearly. You may use them, unchanged, to refer to the site. You may not use them to suggest that the site, or Intersect, has approved, certified, or vouched for anything. That rule follows from what the site is: a record of measurements and methods that draws no conclusions.

## The marks

The files below show the marks; the site's documentation pages carry text only, so they are linked rather than displayed here. The seal is an open ring in Cardano Blue with a check in the portal's ink. The opening in the ring is centered on the line of the check's long stroke; the mark means "checkable", not "approved". The wordmark sets "Verify" in ink and "Cardano" in Cardano Blue as one word, in the same typeface the site uses.

## Using the marks without asking

Accurate, referential uses need no permission: linking to the site, naming it in an article, a filing, a presentation, or a comment with the seal or lockup beside the reference; describing a verification you performed with the site's tools, as long as you say what was verified (that a value matches its ledger commitment) and do not say the site verified your product or claims; citing the site's values and methods under their own licenses; commentary, criticism, teaching, and news; and bookmark or directory icons. Use the files unchanged, keep them separate from your own marks, and do not make them the most prominent element of your material.

## Using the marks only with permission

Ask first before using the name or marks in the name of a product, service, event, organization, publication, account, or domain; on merchandise; in advertising, sponsorship, or fundraising; or in any way that could be read as approval, audit, certification, or endorsement by Intersect or the site.

## Never

Do not alter the marks (geometry, proportions, the gap, the check, the typeface, effects, text inside the ring, rotation, combination with other marks). Do not use the seal, or anything like it, as a badge or stamp of compliance, legality, decentralization, safety, or quality on any asset, product, site, or document. Do not use the marks with material that misrepresents what the site publishes, including a value quoted without its date, methodology version, and reconciliation label where those matter. Do not suggest that the Cardano Foundation, Input Output, or any organization other than Intersect operates or endorses the site. Do not register the name or marks, or anything confusingly similar, as a trademark, company name, or domain.

## Presentation

Colors: ring #0033AD and check #0F172A on light surfaces; ring #8AB0F5 and check white on dark surfaces; one color allowed at 3 to 1 contrast or better. The seal alone is legible at 16 pixels; keep the lockup at least 120 pixels wide. Keep clear space at least the width of the ring's stroke around the seal, and at least the height of the capital V around the lockup. The seal needs no tile or box behind it.

## The Cardano name

"Cardano" is a trademark of the Cardano Foundation. VerifyCardano uses it descriptively, to say what the site measures, under the Foundation's trademark policy. Nothing on this page grants any right in the Cardano name or the Cardano logo, and the VerifyCardano marks never incorporate the Cardano logo or its starburst icon.
