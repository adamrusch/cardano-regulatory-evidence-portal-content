Every metric on the portal is computed under a versioned methodology, and every change of version is a dated entry here with its rationale. The provenance store's methodology versions are the authoritative record: each is content-hashed, immutable once written, and stamped onto every value it produced. This page is the human-readable companion, published from the operator's change log so a reader can see what changed and why without opening a database.

The log records more than definitions. Operator rulings, the documented resolutions of definitional questions with no single defensible answer, are entered here and flagged for ratification by the External Advisory Panel. Withdrawals appear when a metric's definition proved unsettled and publication stopped rather than continuing under a quietly chosen meaning. Corrections appear when something was wrong, including when the something was the operator's own analysis, because entries are append-only in both directions: what was published is never unsaid, and what was wrong is corrected by addition.

Entries are ordered newest first. Methodology version boundaries also appear on each metric's history chart and in the machine-readable data, so a consumer comparing values across time can detect a definition change mechanically rather than by reading prose.
