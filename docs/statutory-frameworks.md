The portal's regulator view organizes the same evidence under three frameworks. This page describes the frameworks themselves and the discipline governing how they are quoted.

## House H.R. 3633, the CLARITY Act

Passed by the US House of Representatives on July 17, 2025. Its Section 205 defines the conditions under which a blockchain system may be certified as a mature blockchain system, including that it is not controlled by any person or group of persons under common control, that it operates programmatically with a verifiable history, and that ownership concentration sits below a statutory threshold, quoted in the mapping as "beneficially owning, in the aggregate, twenty percent or more of the digital commodity supply." The portal maintains this framework as its contingency mapping.

## The Senate merged text

The Senate's market structure counterpart, combining committee versions, remains under negotiation and its enrolled text is not published. Every provision the portal maps under this framework is therefore a paraphrase, described from public reporting and labeled as such. No language under this framework appears in quotation marks, because only an enrolled bill could sustain that discipline.

## The SEC and CFTC joint interpretive release

Release Nos. 33-11412 and 34-105020, adopted March 17, 2026, published at 91 FR 13714. The release classifies crypto assets into five categories and names ADA among its examples of digital commodities, based on characteristics it describes: a functional crypto system, value derived from programmatic operation, and no central party holding operational, economic, or voting control. It is an agency interpretation, not a statute: it governs how the agencies administer the law, does not bind courts, and speaks as of its date. The portal maps its evidence to the release's operative concepts so a reader can check, on any later date, whether the facts behind the classification still hold.

## The citation discipline

Quoted language on the portal reproduces the cited instrument's published text exactly, verified against the source before entry, and everything else is labeled paraphrase. The distinction is shown on every provision panel. Composing statutory-sounding language and marking it verbatim would falsify the record, so the rule is enforced with the same rigor as the numbers.

## Commitments for legislative events

Adopted with the ratified design: within ten days of a bill's passage, the portal confirms its statutory mapping against the enacted text and publishes the confirmation; within thirty days of signature, the mapping is refactored to the enacted structure, which becomes the primary framework. Because the mapping is maintained as data, both are content updates rather than rebuilds.
