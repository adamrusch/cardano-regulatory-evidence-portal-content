The values are published facts, and citing or republishing them with attribution to the portal is welcome; attribution matters because a quoted value carries an evidence tier, a reconciliation label, and a methodology version that a bare number loses. The site's prose content is licensed under CC BY 4.0. A formal license statement for the data files themselves ships with the planned data contract document; until then, treat attribution plus a link as the expected practice.
