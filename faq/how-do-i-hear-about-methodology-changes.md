question: How do I hear about methodology changes?
audience: [builder, academic]
category: consumption
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

Every methodology change is a dated entry in the change log with a version bump that appears on the affected metric's history chart, so consumers comparing values across time can detect definition boundaries mechanically from the methodology version field in the data file. Subscription mechanisms, such as a release feed on the public content repository, are planned as the repositories open up; until then the change log and the version stamps are the reliable signal.
