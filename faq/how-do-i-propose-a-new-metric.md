---

Open an issue with the methodology question template, describing the quantity, the source it can be computed from, and why it is evidence for one of the frameworks' questions. New metrics follow the same discipline as existing ones: a settled methodology with a published rationale, an evidentiary tier, declared sources, and reconciliation where a second source exists. A metric whose definition cannot be settled is published as planned with the reason stated, rather than shipped with a quietly chosen definition.
