question: How do I report an error or dispute a number?
audience: [academic, community, skeptic]
category: contributing
status: locked
publish: false
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

For prose, open an issue on the public content repository using the content bug template: name the page, what it says, what it should say, and your source. For a disputed value, open an issue with your source, the query or method you used, and the date you read it; reproducible disputes are the most useful reports the portal receives, and source disagreements are tracked publicly. Issues are triaged in the open, and the fix, when warranted, is a visible pull request or a logged methodology change, never a silent edit.
