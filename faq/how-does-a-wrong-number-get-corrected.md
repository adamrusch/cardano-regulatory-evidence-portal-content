question: How does a wrong number get corrected?
audience: [regulator, academic, skeptic]
category: methodology
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

By addition, never by erasure. The observation store is append-only: a correction is a new observation that names the observation it corrects, and the original stays in the record with its correction chain attached. Once anchoring is live the same applies on chain, where a correcting anchor supersedes an earlier one without deleting it. What you can never find on this portal is a value that quietly changed.
