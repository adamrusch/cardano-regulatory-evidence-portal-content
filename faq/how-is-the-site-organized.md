Four main views. The overview presents every metric in concept groups, each tile showing the current value, its evidence tier, and its reconciliation status. Evidence and provenance explains how values are produced: the pinned chain tip, the sources read, and the append-only observation store. The regulator view organizes the same metrics under the provisions of three legal frameworks and carries the regulatory recognition ledger at its foot. The FAQ is this page. Every metric tile links to a detail page with the full methodology, caveats, sources, and value history.
