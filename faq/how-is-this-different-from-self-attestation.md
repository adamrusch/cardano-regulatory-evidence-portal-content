The concern is fair, and the design treats it as the central problem rather than an afterthought. Four mitigations: the values are computed from named public sources by published methodologies, so anyone can recompute them; reconciled metrics must agree across independent sources, and disagreements are published; the record is committed on chain so it cannot be rewritten after the fact, and adverse developments are recorded under the same rules as favorable ones; and methodology oversight is assigned to an advisory panel of people unaffiliated with the custodian organizations. What remains true is that the portal is built by Cardano participants; what the design ensures is that a reader never has to take their word for anything.
