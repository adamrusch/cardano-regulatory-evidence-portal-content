question: What are the three evidence tiers?
audience: [regulator, academic, builder]
category: metrics
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

Every metric on this portal carries an evidentiary tier. Tier 1 is a value computable directly from an on-chain source by a query that anyone can reproduce. Tier 2 is a value derived through a heuristic or an indexer where the derivation itself is a modeling choice; the methodology is published so a reader can decide whether they agree with the choice. Tier 3 is an off-chain attested value, published because the attesting party is named and their methodology is on record. The tier travels with the value on every surface.
