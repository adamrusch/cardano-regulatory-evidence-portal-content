Anchoring is a daily commitment of every published value to the Cardano chain. Once anchoring is live, any past value on this site can be verified by an outsider against a hash recorded in a specific block, so the site cannot be silently rewritten. The banner at the top of every page states plainly that anchoring has not begun, because it has not. Until it does, the values here are traceable to their sources but they are not verifiable against an on-chain root, and that difference is important enough that the portal never lets a reader miss it.
