Anchoring is a daily commitment of every published value to the Cardano chain. Any anchored value on this site can be verified by an outsider against a hash recorded in a specific block, so the site cannot be silently rewritten. Mainnet anchoring began on October 18, 2026, and the banner at the top of every page states that start date and the most recent anchor. Values for earlier dates are traceable to their sources but are not verifiable against an on-chain root, and that difference is important enough that the portal never lets a reader miss it.
