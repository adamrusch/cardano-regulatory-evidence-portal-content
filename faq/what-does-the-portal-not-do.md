Four things, by design. It does not certify that any builder's product is compliant with anything; it publishes evidence about the blockchain system, not about products built on it. It does not opine on any person or entity. It does not compute or compare other blockchains' numbers; every value here is about Cardano, and any future comparative content would be attributed third-party research, fenced off from the portal's own record. And it does not give legal or investment advice; it presents measurements with their sources and caveats, and the conclusions belong to the reader.
