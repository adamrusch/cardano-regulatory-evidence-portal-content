question: What happens on the day a bill becomes law?
audience: [regulator, journalist]
category: statutory
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

Two commitments, adopted in advance by the Intersect MBO board so the response is a pre-authorized obligation rather than a decision under pressure. Within ten days of passage, the portal confirms its statutory mapping against the actual enacted text and publishes the confirmation. Within thirty days of signature, the mapping is refactored to the enacted structure, with the enacted framework becoming primary. Because the mapping is maintained as data, both are content updates rather than rebuilds.
