The record so far includes: a governance participation metric withdrawn when its denominator proved ambiguous by a factor of two; a published concentration statistic revised when its population proved misleading, with the change log recording the revision and the reason; and an operator misdiagnosis, where an apparent anomaly in per-epoch values was initially attributed to a computation error, an audit showed the computation was already correct, and the record says plainly that the initial diagnosis was wrong. These entries stay published because the willingness to record error is the portal's strongest evidence of honesty.
