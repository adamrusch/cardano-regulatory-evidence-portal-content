The running record is the methodology change log: every methodology change, withdrawal, and correction is a dated entry with its rationale, including the entries that exist because the operators were wrong. The discipline is append-only in both directions. A wrong value is corrected by a new observation that names what it corrects, and a wrong diagnosis is corrected by an entry that says so, with the original left standing. The portal treats its own record of error as evidence: a site that has never published a correction is not a site that has never been wrong.
