A DRep is a delegated representative under CIP-1694. ADA holders delegate their voting power to a DRep, and that DRep casts votes on governance actions on their behalf. A DRep has two lifecycle states worth naming, because they are often confused. A DRep that stops voting for the drep_activity window becomes inactive: delegations stay in place, the DRep still exists, and it returns to active as soon as it performs an on-chain function such as casting a vote. A DRep is retired only when it publishes an on-chain retirement certificate signed with its keys. Not voting never retires a DRep.
