question: What is an operator ruling?
audience: [academic, regulator, skeptic]

---

Some definitional questions have no single defensible answer: which population counts as active, which epoch a per-epoch aggregate should read, what qualifies for inclusion in a ledger. Rather than deciding silently, the operator records a dated ruling with its reasoning in the methodology change log, and the ruling binds the portal until it is revisited. Rulings are flagged for ratification by the external advisory panel once it is chartered, so judgment calls made by one person carry a visible path to review by people who are not that person.
