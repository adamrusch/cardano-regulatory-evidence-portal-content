CIP-1694 is the Cardano improvement proposal that defines the chain's on-chain governance system, live on mainnet since the Chang hard fork. It distributes governance authority across three bodies: DReps, to whom ADA holders delegate voting power; SPOs, who vote on the action types the constitution assigns them; and a constitutional committee that checks actions against the Cardano constitution. Governance actions, from parameter changes to treasury withdrawals, are submitted, voted, and enacted on chain, which is why this portal can measure governance as observed fact rather than reported process.
