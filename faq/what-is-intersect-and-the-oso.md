Intersect is a member-based organization in the Cardano ecosystem that stewards development coordination and administers ecosystem processes. Its Open Source Office runs the program under which ecosystem open source projects are sponsored, maintained, and reviewed. The Intersect board approved sponsoring this portal through the Open Source Office in August 2026, which brings the repository, its licensing, and its contribution standards under an established program with a named maintainer rather than resting on one individual.
