Intersect is a member-based organization in the Cardano ecosystem that stewards development coordination and administers ecosystem processes. Its Open Source Office runs the program under which ecosystem open source projects are sponsored, maintained, and reviewed. The design the Intersect board ratified on August 5, 2026 places this portal under Open Source Office sponsorship, which brings the repository, its licensing, and its contribution standards under an established program with a named maintainer rather than resting on one individual.
