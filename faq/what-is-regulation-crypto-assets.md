A rule the SEC has proposed but not adopted (Release 33-11434, File No. S7-2026-27): a tailored offering regime for investment contracts involving crypto assets, with a startup exemption, a fundraising exemption, and an investment contract safe harbor. The safe harbor would codify the joint interpretation's standard as Rule 400: an issuer that has completed or permanently ceased all the essential managerial efforts it promised, and makes no new promises, files a transition report certifying that with a supporting analysis, and the crypto asset is deemed no longer subject to the investment contract. The proposal adds no network-level tests: no ownership threshold, no decentralization criteria, no required measurements. Because it is proposed rather than final, it changes nothing this portal maps; the joint interpretive release remains the operative administrative framework, and this entry will be revised if the rule is adopted. The statutory frameworks page carries the fuller description.
