The CLARITY Act, H.R. 3633, is digital asset market structure legislation passed by the US House of Representatives in July 2025, with a Senate counterpart in negotiation. Its Section 205 defines conditions under which a blockchain system can be certified as a mature blockchain system: not controlled by any person or group under common control, operating programmatically with a verifiable history, and with ownership distribution below a statutory threshold.
