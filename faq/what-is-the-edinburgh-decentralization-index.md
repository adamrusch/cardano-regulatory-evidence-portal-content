---

A research program at the University of Edinburgh that measures blockchain decentralization with published academic methodology, including mappings of which stake pools are operated by the same entity. The portal uses the index's cluster mapping as the basis for one of its operator-level concentration readings, labeled as tier 3 evidence because the mapping is an attested external methodology rather than an on-chain fact. The portal also publishes an on-chain-only operator reading alongside it, so a reader can see what each method can and cannot detect.
