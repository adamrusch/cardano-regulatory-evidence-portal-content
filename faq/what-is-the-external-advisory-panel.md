A planned body of three to five members unaffiliated with the organizations that hold anchoring custody. Its role is methodology oversight: ratifying or overturning the operator rulings that resolve definitional judgment calls, and gating content categories that need independent judgment, such as heuristic ownership figures or any comparative material. Its chartering was requested of and approved by the Intersect MBO board; recruiting takes time, and until it is seated, rulings are recorded with an explicit flag that panel ratification is pending. A dedicated page on the panel is planned with the documentation section.
