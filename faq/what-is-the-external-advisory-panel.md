A body of three to five members unaffiliated with the organizations that hold anchoring custody, provided for by the design the Intersect MBO board ratified. Its role is methodology oversight: ratifying or overturning the operator rulings that resolve definitional judgment calls, and gating content categories that need independent judgment, such as heuristic ownership figures or any comparative material. Until the panel is seated, rulings are recorded with an explicit flag that panel ratification is pending.
