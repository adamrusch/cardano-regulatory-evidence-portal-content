In March 2026 the SEC and CFTC jointly published an interpretive release, Release 33-11412, that classifies crypto assets into five categories, and it names ADA among its examples of digital commodities. The classification was made as of the release date, based on characteristics the release describes: a functional crypto system, value derived from programmatic operation, and no central party holding operational, economic, or voting control. The regulator view offers the release as a third framework, mapping the same metrics to those concepts, so a reader can check whether the facts behind the classification still hold on any later date. The release is an agency interpretation rather than a statute: it guides how the agencies administer the law, it does not bind courts, and it can be revised. The portal treats it the way it treats every other claim, as something to keep verifiable rather than something to take on faith.
