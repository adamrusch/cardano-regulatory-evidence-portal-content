question: What is the recognition ledger, and what qualifies as an entry?
audience: [regulator, journalist]
category: recognition-ledger
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

An append-only, dated record of formal acts by named authorities that concern Cardano or ADA specifically, kept at the foot of the regulator view. The inclusion rule is narrow: statutes, rules and interpretive releases, orders, no-action letters, and product listings on regulated exchanges qualify; speeches, press coverage, and pending bills do not. Each entry carries the instrument, its date, a citation, a link to the primary source, and either an exactly verified quotation or a clearly labeled description. The ledger records facts of recognition, not endorsements.
