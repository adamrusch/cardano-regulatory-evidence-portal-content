This site is an evidence portal. It presents measurements about the Cardano blockchain system and, for each measurement, the sources it came from, the reconciliation the pipeline performed, and the caveats that qualify it. It does not assert any regulatory status for Cardano, and nothing on the page is a legal opinion. A reader is expected to draw their own conclusions from evidence that is stated in the open and, once anchoring begins, verifiable against the chain.
