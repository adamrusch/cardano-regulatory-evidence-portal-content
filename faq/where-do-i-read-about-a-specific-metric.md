Click any metric tile, on the overview or in the regulator view, to reach its detail page. The detail page carries the plain-language reading of the current value, the methodology prose, the computation description, every caveat, the sources read on the latest run, the value history with methodology-version boundaries marked, and the statutory provisions the metric responds to.
