The rule is fixed by a documented operator ruling recorded in the methodology change log, and it is flagged for ratification by the External Advisory Panel the ratified design provides for. Changing the rule would itself be a new dated ruling, in public, and entries admitted under the old rule would remain in the record; the ledger is append-only, including about itself.
