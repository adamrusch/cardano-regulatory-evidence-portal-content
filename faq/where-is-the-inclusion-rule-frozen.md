audience: [skeptic, academic]

The ledger and its inclusion rule were adopted by a documented operator ruling in August 2026, recorded in the methodology change log, with the rule fixed at adoption. The ruling is flagged for ratification by the External Advisory Panel once it is chartered. Changing the rule would itself be a new dated ruling, in public, and entries admitted under the old rule would remain in the record; the ledger is append-only, including about itself.
