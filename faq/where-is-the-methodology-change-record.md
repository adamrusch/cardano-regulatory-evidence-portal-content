question: Where is the record of every methodology change?
audience: [academic, regulator]
category: methodology
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

Two places, one authoritative and one readable. Authoritatively, every methodology version is a content-hashed, immutable row in the portal's provenance store, and every published value carries its methodology version. Readably, a methodology change log records each change with its date, the version bump, and a one-paragraph rationale, including the changes that were prompted by being wrong. The log is publishing as part of the documentation section; methodology-version boundaries are already marked on every value history chart.
