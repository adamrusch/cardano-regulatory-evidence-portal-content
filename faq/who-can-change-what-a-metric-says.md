question: Who can change what a metric says, and who cannot?
audience: [regulator, skeptic]
category: governance
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

A metric's definition changes only through a versioned methodology change with a published rationale, and the version travels with every value it produced. The operator can propose and enact changes, but cannot do so invisibly: the change log, the version stamps on every chart, and eventually the anchored methodology hashes make every change a public event. No sponsor, custodian, or funder holds any mechanism to alter a published value; the append-only store and, once live, the on-chain anchors make retroactive editing detectable by anyone.
