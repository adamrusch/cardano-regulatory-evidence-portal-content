The development of this portal was sponsored by Intersect MBO. The design ratified by the Intersect MBO board places it under sponsorship of Intersect's Open Source Office, which brings its maintenance under an established program. Any funding arrangement in force will be stated on this page, because a reader weighing the evidence is entitled to know who pays for the scale.
