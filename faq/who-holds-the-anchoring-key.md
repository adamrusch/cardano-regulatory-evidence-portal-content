Daily anchoring uses an operational hot key whose authority is established, and revocable, through an anchored key registry. The design ratified by the Intersect MBO board moves rotation authority after launch to a two-of-three multisignature scheme held across Intersect, IOG, and the Cardano Foundation, so no single organization can forge or suppress the record. Custody is deliberately separate from methodology oversight: the organizations securing the record hold no authority over what it says.
