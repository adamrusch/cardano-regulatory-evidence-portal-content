Every content change is reviewed by a maintainer, and files carrying methodology-relevant or legally sensitive prose additionally require the domain reviewer named in the repository's code owners file: the operator of record for methodology pages, with the External Advisory Panel chair added once the panel is seated. Style rules are enforced by automation rather than reviewer discretion, so review attention goes to substance.
