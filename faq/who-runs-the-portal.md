The portal operates under a design ratified by the board of Intersect MBO, one of Cardano's member-based organizations, on August 5, 2026. Day to day it is run by its founding maintainer under that design's governance model: every methodology change is a versioned entry with a published rationale, definitional judgment calls are resolved by documented operator rulings, and corrections are made in public. The ratified design provides for code stewardship under Intersect's Open Source Office with a named maintainer, and for an External Advisory Panel of unaffiliated members holding methodology oversight.
