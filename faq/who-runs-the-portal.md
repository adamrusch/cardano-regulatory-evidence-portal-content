The portal was built and is operated by its founding maintainer, Adam P. Rusch, under a governance model designed to outgrow him. Every methodology change is a versioned entry with a published rationale, definitional judgment calls are resolved by documented operator rulings, and corrections are made in public. In August 2026 the board of Intersect MBO, one of Cardano's member-based organizations, approved sponsoring the portal through its Open Source Office with a named maintainer, and chartering an external advisory panel of unaffiliated members to hold methodology oversight. A dedicated governance page with the full model is publishing with the documentation section of this site.
