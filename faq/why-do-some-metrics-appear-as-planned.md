question: Why do some metrics appear as planned rather than showing a number?
audience: [regulator, builder, academic]
category: metrics
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

A metric is published only when its methodology is settled. When a metric is listed but has no value, the tile shows planned and the metric page states the reason: usually that the denominator is contested, that the source does not expose the aggregate we need, or that the candidate definitions give materially different answers on live data. The planned tiles are shown rather than hidden because a labeled gap is more honest than a blank space that quietly omits the question.
