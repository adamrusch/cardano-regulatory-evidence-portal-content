Because the organizations that secure the record must not control what it says. The planned custody scheme puts anchor key rotation authority under a two-of-three arrangement across Intersect, IOG, and the Cardano Foundation, three organizations with an interest in Cardano. Methodology oversight sits with the unaffiliated advisory panel precisely because those custodians are interested parties. A reader is never asked to trust that the interested organizations grade their own homework; the separation is the design answer to that objection.
