question: Why does the regulator view show three frameworks?
audience: [regulator, academic]
category: statutory
status: locked

---

Because the legal landscape has three live tracks and a reader should not have to guess which one applies. The Senate merged text is the operative bill under negotiation; House H.R. 3633 Section 205 is the passed text the Senate work builds on, kept as the contingency mapping; and the SEC and CFTC joint interpretive release of March 2026 is the administrative framework already in force while legislation is pending. All three organize the same underlying metrics; only the grouping and the provision language differ. When an enrolled bill exists, its framework becomes primary.
