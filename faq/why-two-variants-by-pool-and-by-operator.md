question: Why is the same statistic shown in by-pool and by-operator variants?
audience: [regulator, academic]
category: metrics
status: locked
last_reviewed: "2026-08-08"
---

Because they answer different questions. A stake pool is an on-chain entity; an operator is a person or company that may run many pools. Concentration measured by pool answers how the protocol's own units of block production are distributed. Concentration measured by operator asks who could actually coordinate, which is the question control tests care about, and it requires clustering evidence that on-chain data alone cannot fully provide. The portal publishes both, with the operator-level readings labeled by their clustering method and candid about what the method cannot detect. Publishing only the flattering variant would be an editorial choice; publishing both is evidence.
